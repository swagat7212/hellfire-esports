How to join

Your squad's entry process

  1. Choose your mode, then enter your player / team name and captain's WhatsApp number.
  2. Pay the entry fee shown for your selected mode to the displayed UPI ID.
  3. Enter the UTR / transaction reference.
  4. Admin verifies payment and allots a slot.
  5. After approval, your WhatsApp group invite appears in registration status.
Registrations are stored in the shared online system. Payment is still verified manually by the admin.
